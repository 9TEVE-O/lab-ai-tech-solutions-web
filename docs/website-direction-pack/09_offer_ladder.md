# LAB Offer Ladder

## Purpose

This file turns LAB’s website positioning into clear buyer-facing offers.

The offer ladder should help the website move from:

> “Here is what LAB believes.”

To:

> “Here is how to engage safely.”

---

## Commercial principle

**One painful workflow. One visible demo. One buyer-facing artefact. One safe next step.**

The offer ladder should not make LAB look like a generic AI automation agency.

It should make LAB look like a practical AI systems company focused on safer document workflows, evidence trails, and human approval gates.

---

# Ladder overview

| Level | Offer | Purpose | Buyer readiness |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI-Safe Document Intake Audit | Diagnose one risky document workflow | Early / unsure |
| 2 | Evidence Layer Audit | Map evidence, claims, risks, and approval gaps | Aware / governance-conscious |
| 3 | Safe Intake Workflow Prototype | Build a small controlled demo around one workflow | Ready to test |
| 4 | AI Knowledge System Readiness Sprint | Prepare approved content for retrieval/search | Ready to implement |

# Offer 1 — AI-Safe Document Intake Audit

## Position

A practical audit of one document workflow before AI tools are introduced.

## Best buyer

- document-heavy small business
- music or creative organisation
- community organisation
- consultant or operator using sensitive records
- team considering AI search, summarisation, or knowledge-base tools

## Buyer problem

The team wants to use AI, but cannot clearly answer:

- what documents are received
- where originals are stored
- what sensitive information exists
- who reviews documents
- what is approved for AI use
- what is blocked
- what evidence exists after the workflow runs

## Outcome

A clear map of current intake risk and a practical next-step plan.

## Deliverables

- current workflow map
- document-risk inventory
- sensitivity and redaction gap review
- AI-access boundary recommendation
- human review checkpoint recommendation
- audit trail recommendation
- one-page client-ready summary
- optional sample Safe Intake flow for one document type

## Out of scope

- legal compliance certification
- security audit of all systems
- production backend build
- live AI ingestion of sensitive documents
- migration of all documents
- automated compliance claims

## Website CTA

```text
Request an AI-Safe Document Intake Audit
```

## Safer website copy

> LAB reviews one document workflow and shows where sensitive information enters, where review is missing, what should be blocked from AI, and what evidence trail should exist before automation is added.

# Offer 2 — Evidence Layer Audit

## Position

A review of whether a team can prove what its AI-assisted workflow used, skipped, inferred, blocked, and approved.

## Best buyer

- teams already experimenting with AI
- organisations with document-heavy internal knowledge
- governance-aware founders or operators
- teams needing source-backed outputs
- teams worried about hallucinations, privacy, or unsupported claims

## Buyer problem

The team has AI outputs, but cannot show:

- source evidence
- claim status
- review decisions
- risk flags
- unsupported assumptions
- blocked content
- final approval boundary

## Outcome

A practical evidence model for AI-supported work.

## Deliverables

- source evidence map
- claim ledger model
- risk register model
- review and approval map
- blocked-content policy recommendation
- sample output evidence standard
- website/internal-use proof artefact

## Out of scope

- guarantee that outputs are correct
- legal or regulatory sign-off
- full AI governance program
- production observability platform
- enterprise policy suite

## Website CTA

```text
Book an Evidence Layer Audit
```

## Safer website copy

> LAB helps teams make AI-assisted work easier to inspect by mapping evidence, claims, risks, review gates, and approved outputs.

# Offer 3 — Safe Intake Workflow Prototype

## Position

A small prototype showing how one document type could move through intake, classification, redaction, review, approval, and audit logging.

## Best buyer

- buyer has a specific document workflow
- buyer understands the risk
- buyer wants proof before committing to a larger build
- buyer can provide synthetic or de-identified examples

## Buyer problem

The team needs to see how Safe Intake would work in practice before investing in production implementation.

## Outcome

A working prototype or clickable demo for one workflow.

## Deliverables

- workflow state model
- sample document path
- classification logic draft
- sensitive-field detection rules
- redacted candidate screen
- human review checkpoint
- approved redacted copy boundary
- audit receipt output
- prototype handover notes

## Out of scope

- production document storage
- handling real sensitive documents without controls
- automatic legal/compliance approval
- full role-based access control
- enterprise deployment
- model training on client documents

## Website CTA

```text
Prototype one Safe Intake workflow
```

## Safer website copy

> LAB can prototype a controlled Safe Intake workflow around one document type so the team can see the review gate, redaction boundary, and audit receipt before building a production system.

# Offer 4 — AI Knowledge System Readiness Sprint

## Position

Prepare approved content for AI search, summarisation, or internal knowledge-base workflows.

## Best buyer

- team has documents they want to make searchable
- team needs source-backed answers
- team has approval and privacy concerns
- team wants to avoid dumping raw folders into AI tools

## Buyer problem

The team wants a knowledge system, but its documents are messy, sensitive, duplicated, unapproved, or unsupported by evidence metadata.

## Outcome

A structured readiness layer before retrieval, indexing, or AI-assisted answering.

## Deliverables

- content inventory
- document readiness scoring
- approved-source list
- exclusion/block list
- redaction requirements
- metadata plan
- retrieval-readiness recommendation
- citation and answer-quality standard

## Out of scope

- guaranteed answer accuracy
- full RAG platform build unless separately scoped
- migration of every historical document
- legal classification of all records
- storing raw sensitive data in vector indexes

## Website CTA

```text
Prepare your knowledge base for AI
```

## Safer website copy

> LAB helps teams prepare approved, lower-risk content before building AI search or knowledge-base workflows.

# Recommended website order

## Homepage offer strip

Use three cards only:

1. **AI-Safe Document Intake Audit**
2. **Safe Intake Workflow Prototype**
3. **AI Knowledge System Readiness Sprint**

Keep Evidence Layer Audit as a deeper page or secondary offer unless the buyer is governance-aware.

---

# Service page structure

Each offer page should use this format:

```text
1. Who it is for
2. The problem
3. What LAB reviews or builds
4. What you receive
5. What this does not claim
6. The safe next step
```

---

# Lead qualification questions

Use these in contact form or sales call:

1. What document workflow are you trying to improve?
2. What types of documents are involved?
3. Do the documents contain personal, financial, health, legal, client, or employee information?
4. Where are originals currently stored?
5. Who reviews or approves documents today?
6. Are you already using AI tools with these documents?
7. What would a safe first proof look like?
8. Are you able to provide synthetic or de-identified samples?

---

# Red flags

Do not proceed casually if the buyer wants:

- to upload real sensitive documents into a public demo
- to bypass human review
- to automate legal, medical, financial, or employment decisions
- to claim compliance without evidence
- to index raw originals
- to use AI as the only reviewer
- to train models on sensitive documents without a clear policy

Use this response:

> That needs a safer scope. LAB can start by mapping the workflow, identifying sensitive data, and defining the approval and evidence boundaries before any AI ingestion occurs.

---

# Pricing placeholders

Do not publish exact prices until the service scope is stable.

Use:

- “Fixed-scope audit available”
- “Prototype scoped after audit”
- “Implementation quoted after workflow review”

Avoid:

- “from $X” unless pricing is committed
- “enterprise-grade”
- “guaranteed ROI”
- “fully compliant”
