## Design Direction

The site should feel:

- calm
- technical
- trustworthy
- evidence-first
- premium
- deliberate

Use:

- document cards
- workflow states
- approval gates
- evidence paths
- reasoning maps
- audit markers
- calm motion
- editorial typography
- modernist structure

Avoid:

- neon AI gradients
- robot graphics as primary brand imagery
- floating AI particles
- holograms
- generic SaaS dashboards
- meaningless decorative effects
- fake metrics
- fake testimonials
- fake client logos

---

## Character System Use

The guide character can appear only as a secondary explanatory device.

It may explain the system.

It may not become the evidence for the system.

Do not place the character in the hero as the main visual.

---

## Quality Gate

Before finalising, check:

1. Can a mobile visitor explain what LAB does within 30 seconds?
2. Is Safe Intake Proof 001 clearly the primary proof?
3. Is there one clear path to engagement?
4. Are there any unsupported claims?
5. Is the site becoming a philosophy page instead of a business asset?
6. Has anything been invented?
7. Does every section increase trust, provide proof, or move the visitor towards engagement?

If not, simplify.

---

## Final Build Principle

Problem → Proof → Trust → Conversation.

Do not optimise only for understanding.

Optimise for action.
