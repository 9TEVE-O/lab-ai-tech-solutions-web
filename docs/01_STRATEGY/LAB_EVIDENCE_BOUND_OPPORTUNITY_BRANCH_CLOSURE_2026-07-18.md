## Retained finding

The larger system discovered by the responsive redesign is a governed capability graph that links evidence, provenance, lifecycle, authority, permitted claims, opportunity posture, decision ownership, reviews and state transitions.

Working label: **Evidence-Bound Opportunity Operating System**.

This label remains an architectural hypothesis, not a product declaration.

## Known structural corrections

Two corrections were identified but are not implemented in this closed branch:

1. formalise a shared metadata envelope so record metadata does not collide with domain-specific status fields;
2. define an explicit initialisation receipt for converting an existing unstructured record into its first governed state.

These corrections may be applied in a future `LAB_EVIDENCE_BOUND_OPPORTUNITY_SCHEMA_v0.2.md` only after explicit reactivation.

## Reactivation gate

Do not reopen this branch for visual polish or feature expansion alone.

Reactivation requires one of the following:

- a decision to encode a second materially different inventory;
- a need to implement the two identified schema corrections;
- a real workflow requiring governed claim, opportunity or lifecycle decisions; or
- evidence that the model should become an operational LAB system.

## Final decision

Goal complete. Preserve the artefacts, keep the prototype internal, and do not extend the system further without a new decision and evidence-backed use case.
