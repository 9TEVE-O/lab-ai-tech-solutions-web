## Non-Negotiable Messaging

- Most organisations do not have an AI problem first.
- They have an evidence problem.
- No approval = no AI access.
- Before information reaches AI systems, it should be classified, reviewed, approved, and traceable.
- Safe Intake Proof 001 demonstrates that principle in practice.

## Brand Role Split

### Symbol

The symbol represents the system:

- structure
- flow
- signal
- transformation
- architecture

### CEO Millennial Uncle / Guide Character

The character represents the guide:

- understanding
- calmness
- judgement
- practicality
- human review

The guide character may explain the system.

The guide character may never become the evidence for the system.
