## Working website position

LAB AI & Tech Solutions helps teams make AI-assisted workflows clearer, traceable and human-controlled before automation expands.

This is working positioning, not automatic publication approval.

## Primary public offer

**AI Workflow Review**

A practical review of one defined AI-assisted workflow. It examines where AI touches source material, documents, tools, decisions, approvals, evidence, logging, failure handling and rollback.

## Follow-on boundary

A bounded implementation may be scoped after the review, based on the evidence and control gaps identified.

Do not publish a broad implementation promise without later evidence/approval.

## Proof position

**Safe Intake Proof 001** is a completed internal demonstration of controlled document intake and approval boundaries.

It may support method credibility but does not establish client outcomes, production readiness, compliance, security certification or universal effectiveness.

## Primary conversion

One qualified email enquiry about one defined AI-assisted workflow.

Do not require or invite sensitive documents in the initial enquiry.
