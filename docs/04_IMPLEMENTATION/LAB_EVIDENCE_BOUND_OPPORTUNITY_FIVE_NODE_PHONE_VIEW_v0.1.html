Evidence-Bound Opportunity Test

Purpose and authority boundary: This is a five-node internal validation view. It does not prove clients, revenue, production readiness, market demand, legal authority, clinical authority or approved services.

Strategic decision

Use capability, evidence, authority, claim and lifecycle records to decide what LAB may safely say or pursue. Do not turn the five examples into five service claims.

How to read the states

  • Evidence class describes the material available.
  • Lifecycle describes organisational attention.
  • Commercial-offer state is separate from both.
  • None of these states alone proves a client, outcome, authority or market.

Claim controls

Permitted: narrow wording tied to the visible support statement and authority boundary.

Prohibited: inferring approved services, outcomes, certification, sector-wide delivery or demand from a badge or count.

Fixture distribution

Five representative records: one active built capability, one active direct-evidence node, one completed direct-evidence node, one parked research node and one parked idea-only node. This is test coverage, not market size or priority.

Domain coverage

AI and software systems; document-heavy and regulated workflows; events, logistics and public-sector operations. Domain presence is not a strength score.

Transition and review controls

Every node has a human decision owner, a claim-status receipt and a review trigger. Parked and completed records do not reactivate through display, retrieval or reuse. High-stakes records suspend claim use when their blocking trigger fires.
