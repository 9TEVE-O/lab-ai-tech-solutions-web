## Prompt

Assume this work is accidentally discovering something much larger than the authors believe they are studying.

What is the larger thing?

---

## Working Answer

The larger thing is not only Safe Intake, AI workflow, or document governance.

The larger thing is **Cognitive Infrastructure Governance**: the governance of the information environments that AI systems think, remember, retrieve, and act inside.

---

## Core Thesis

> In the AI era, ingestion is governance.

Whoever controls intake controls the future behaviour of the system because they control the memory, evidence, permissions, and retrieval surface around the model.

---

## Key Shift

The boundary is no longer only:

```text
Can AI read this?
```

The stronger boundary is:

```text
Can this become part of what machines remember, retrieve, act on, and cite later?
```

---

## Hidden Object

The work appears to study document intake.

Underneath that, it studies how information becomes machine-actionable authority.

A document can now become:

- searchable memory
- retrieval context
- agent instruction
- compliance evidence
- decision substrate
- operational trigger
- future justification
- automated action input
