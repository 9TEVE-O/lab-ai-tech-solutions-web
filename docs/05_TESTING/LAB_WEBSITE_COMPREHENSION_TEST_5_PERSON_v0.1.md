# LAB Website — 5-Person Comprehension Test v0.1

**Build:** `staging/safe-intake-comprehension-001`  
**Pages under test:** Homepage + Safe Intake Proof 001  
**Purpose:** Test whether a first-time visitor can understand LAB's proposition, proof status, differentiation, limitations, and engagement path without explanation.  
**Test size:** 5 people  
**Status:** Staging test protocol

## Test rule

Do not explain LAB, Safe Intake, the website strategy, or the intended answers before the participant responds.

Ask the participant to open the homepage and look through the site naturally. If they ask what they are supposed to do, say only:

> Have a look as if someone had sent you this website and you wanted to understand what the business does.

Do not coach them toward Safe Intake.

## Core questions

Ask these in order after the participant has viewed the homepage and Safe Intake page.

1. In your own words, what does LAB AI & Tech Solutions do?
2. What seems different about LAB compared with a generic AI consultant or AI agency?
3. What is Safe Intake Proof 001, and what do you think it actually proves?
4. What does Safe Intake Proof 001 **not** prove or do yet?
5. If you had an AI-related workflow problem, would you know what to do next from this website? What would you do?

## Recall check

Without letting the participant reopen the page, ask:

- What phrase or idea do you remember most clearly?
- What part was confusing or required the most effort to understand?
- Was anything presented as more mature or complete than you think it really is?

## Behaviour check

Record whether the participant independently:

- noticed the `Working proof` status;
- opened the Safe Intake page;
- understood that AI ingestion is blocked in the current proof;
- understood that `APPROVED` / `REJECTED` are modelled but not enabled in Proof 001;
- found the contact/engagement direction;
- confused Safe Intake with a production product, secure upload service, compliance product, or client case study.

## Scoring

Score each participant from 0–2 on the five core comprehension outcomes.

| Outcome | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understands what LAB does | Incorrect / unclear | Partly correct | Clear and materially correct |
| Understands differentiation | Generic AI / automation framing | Some evidence/control distinction | Evidence, boundaries, review/human judgement clearly recognised |
| Understands Safe Intake | Treats as product/platform or unclear | Understands controlled intake broadly | Understands bounded proof and current stop at human review |
| Understands limitations | Misses or contradicts limits | Recalls some limits | Clearly understands material non-claims |
| Understands engagement path | No idea what to do | General contact idea | Can describe the intended next action and sensitive-data boundary |

**Maximum per person:** 10  
**Maximum across five people:** 50

## Decision gate

### Retain the structure

Use when:

- at least 4/5 participants can explain what LAB does materially correctly;
- at least 4/5 understand Safe Intake as a bounded proof rather than a production product;
- at least 4/5 recognise one meaningful difference based on evidence, boundaries, review, or human judgement;
- no more than 1/5 materially overestimates the proof's maturity.

### Revise the page hierarchy or copy

Use when:

- 2 or more participants think Safe Intake is production software, a secure upload product, a compliance product, or a client case study;
- 2 or more cannot explain LAB after viewing both pages;
- limitations are consistently missed;
- the proof detail overwhelms the business proposition;
- the site is understood as internal governance documentation rather than a public evidence interface.

### Do not expand the website yet

If the comprehension gate fails, revise only the failed hierarchy/copy elements and rerun a small test. Do not respond by adding more pages, features, frameworks, animations, or proof concepts.
