# Safe Intake Proof 001

Status: flagship proof artefact for LAB AI & Tech Solutions website.

---

## Public Positioning

Safe Intake Proof 001 is LAB's first working proof.

It demonstrates how documents can move through classification, review, approval, and evidence-tracking stages before being used with AI systems.

---

## What It Is

A controlled document-intake proof demonstrating:

- document intake
- classification
- quarantine
- review states
- approval gates
- evidence ledger events
- AI-ingestion blocking until approval
- controlled workflow transitions

---

## What It Is Not

Do not present Safe Intake Proof 001 as:

- production software
- compliance software
- enterprise platform
- completed product
- guaranteed risk elimination
- certified security or legal solution

---

## Core Principle

> Before information reaches AI systems, it should be classified, reviewed, approved, and traceable.

Safe Intake Proof 001 demonstrates that principle in practice.

---

## Homepage Version
