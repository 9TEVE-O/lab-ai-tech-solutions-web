# LAB Website Publication Boundary v1.0

**Status:** Stage 0 control document  
**Date:** 7 August 2026  
**Controlling pack:** `LAB_AI_WEBSITE_MASTER_BUILD_PACK_v1.2.md`

## Purpose

Prevent repository history, proofs, internal controls, draft visual systems and unverified claims from being promoted into public website content without explicit evidence and approval.

## Allowed public direction

The public site may describe:

- LAB AI & Tech Solutions as a practical AI workflow consulting/development business;
- AI Workflow Review as the primary working offer, subject to final copy approval;
- Safe Intake Proof 001 as a completed internal demonstration with explicit limitations;
- plain-English workflow, evidence, authority, review, failure and rollback concepts;
- bounded implementation follow-on only where the approved wording permits it;
- contact through an approved email-only enquiry path.

## Must remain non-public or bounded

Do not publish as current authority or active offer:

- `AI-Safe Document Intake Audit` as the primary offer;
- Agent-Ready Website / Agent Access Layer;
- Agent-Ready Website Pack Generator;
- `LabLLM` proof interface unless separately reviewed and authorised;
- old Safe Intake-first homepage positioning;
- historical builder/system prompts;
- internal control documents;
- draft or non-adopted Visual Evidence Grammar as canonical methodology;
- internal `ReasoningMap` terminology as established public LAB methodology unless separately approved;
- older portfolio/recruiter data or components;
- internal skills/evidence registries unless explicitly cleared.

## Claim boundaries

Do not publish unsupported statements implying:

- client outcomes;
- production readiness;
- compliance or certification;
- enterprise security status;
- universal safety/effectiveness;
- guaranteed productivity or business improvement;
- clients, testimonials, partnerships, revenue, metrics or credentials without evidence;
- live AI reasoning merely because an interface animates or responds.

## Proof boundary

Safe Intake Proof 001 must be visibly labelled as:

- completed internal demonstration;
- not a client case study;
- not production software;
- not compliance/security certification;
- not evidence of business outcomes.

Any visual, mock, sample or interactive workflow that is illustrative/synthetic must be labelled accordingly where a reasonable viewer could mistake it for deployed evidence.

## Privacy boundary

- No initial sensitive-document intake.
- No data-collecting contact form unless separately approved.
- Use a configuration-controlled public email address.
- Initial enquiry copy must warn users not to send sensitive documents.
- No third-party tracking by default.
- No secret or API key in client-side code.

## Release-blocking publication conditions

Publication must fail closed when:

- any stale offer/generator/unauthorised proof surface remains publicly reachable;
- an unsupported public claim remains;
- unresolved placeholder tokens remain in the built artefact;
- approved contact email/domain/privacy/terms decisions are missing;
- required routes or enquiry behaviour fail;
- accessibility or Rubric OS release gates fail;
- the exact staged release candidate lacks explicit Human Decision Record approval.
