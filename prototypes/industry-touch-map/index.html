State model

Evidence and lifecycle remain separate.

A completed sector can still have strong direct evidence. An active sector may have only research. One state must never be used as a proxy for the other.

Evidence state

Direct evidence
Operational, client-related, case-study or professional material exists. This does not automatically prove a paid LAB engagement.
Built capability
LAB produced a reusable system, package, prototype, method, standard or product relevant to the sector.
Research
Substantive research, analysis or framework development exists, but not sector delivery proof.
Idea only
A documented concept exists without sufficient build, research or delivery evidence.

Lifecycle state

Active
Currently relevant to LAB’s operating direction, research, product work or opportunity development.
Completed
A bounded capability, project or artefact was completed and may be reused as proof of capability or reference.
Parked
Deliberately held outside active execution. Reactivation requires an explicit decision.
Archived
Retained as historical, superseded or legacy reference. It is not current authority.

Claim control

Keep the map useful without turning it into inflated positioning.

Internal wording

LAB AI & Tech Solutions produced work, systems, research, packages, prototypes or documented ideas touching 20 consolidated industry groups between 19 April and 18 July 2026.

Public-safe wording

LAB’s experience and research connect five practical domains: AI and software systems; document-heavy and regulated workflows; music, arts and creative operations; events, logistics and public-sector operations; and education, research and knowledge systems.

Do not infer that LAB

  • serves, has clients in or has earned revenue from twenty industries;
  • holds formal authority, certification or professional registration across the mapped sectors;
  • has validated demand or product-market fit across the map; or
  • actively offers every concept or capability listed.

Control and review

A sector changes state only through a new evidence record.

This dated snapshot does not reactivate completed, parked, archived or superseded work. A state-change record must identify:

  1. the evidence added;
  2. the current authority source;
  3. the buyer or user being served;
  4. the bounded offer or use case;
  5. the risks and exclusions;
  6. the decision owner; and
  7. the new lifecycle state.

Next review trigger: material buyer evidence, a completed sector-specific delivery, a public positioning change, or 90 days from this snapshot.
